# Wang et al. (2018) - Equivalent Temperature Index for dairy Cattle (ETIC)

## Air speed cooling used by this controller
The controller treats moving air as lowering the temperature the cow feels by 0.1173 x v^0.707 x (39.2 - T) degrees C, where v is air speed in m/s and T is air temperature in C. At 28 C it is worth about 1.8 THI points at 1 m/s and 3.0 THI points at 2 m/s. The cooling shrinks as air temperature approaches cow body temperature (39.2 C).

Source: Wang et al. (2018), A predictive model of equivalent temperature index for dairy cattle (ETIC), J. Thermal Biology (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0306456518301050)
