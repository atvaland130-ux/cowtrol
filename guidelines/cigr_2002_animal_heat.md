# CIGR (2002) - Heat and moisture production at animal and house levels

## Clustering heat estimate
Clustering is measured as the average number of other cows within about one cow length (2.5 m). The controller assumes each nearby cow's body heat (about 500 W of sensible heat) warms the air reaching its neighbours. The warming is n x 500 / (1.2 x 1005 x 3 x v) degrees C for n nearby cows and air speed v (never below 0.2 m/s): roughly 1 THI point per nearby cow in still air and 0.2 THI points at 1 m/s, so moving air matters more when cows are bunched. This is an engineering estimate, not a measured relationship.

Source: CIGR (2002), Heat and moisture production at animal and house levels (https://www.cigr.org/sites/default/files/documets/CIGR_4TH_WORK_GR.pdf)
