# University of Kentucky - Fans: How Cool Are Your Cows

## Target air speed over cows
University of Kentucky guidance targets about 7.2 ft/s over cows, which is about 4.9 mph.

## Fan turn-on temperature
Thermostat-controlled fans should turn on when the temperature at cow level reaches about 73 F.

## When fans are not enough
Air movement alone helps at the start of heat stress, but once cows reach more severe heat stress, convective cooling may not be sufficient and evaporative cooling (soaking cows with sprinklers plus fans) is recommended.

Source: University of Kentucky, Fans: how cool are your cows (https://afs.mgcafe.uky.edu/files/fans_how_cool_are_your_cows.pdf)
