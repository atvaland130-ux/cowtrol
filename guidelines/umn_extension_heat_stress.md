# UMN Extension - Heat Stress in Dairy Cattle

## THI stress categories
UMN Extension groups heat stress by THI: below 68 is no stress; 68 to 71 is mild; 72 to 79 is mild to moderate; 80 to 90 is moderate to severe; 90 to 99 is severe. Respiration rate and body temperature rise with each category, from roughly 40 to 60 breaths per minute with no stress to over 100 breaths per minute when severe.

## Effect of higher air speed
At THI 75, raising air speed from about 3 mph to 10 mph lowered a cow's respiration rate from 68 to 57 breaths per minute, moving it from mild stress to normal.

## Emergency signs
Take immediate action when more than about 10% of cows breathe faster than 100 breaths per minute, or when more than 5 to 10% have body temperatures above 105 F.

Source: UMN Extension, Heat stress in dairy cattle (https://extension.umn.edu/dairy-milking-cows/heat-stress-dairy-cattle)
