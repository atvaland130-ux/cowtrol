# Mader et al. (2006) - Adjusted THI for feedlot cattle

## Wind and solar adjustment to THI
For feedlot cattle, the adjusted THI subtracts 1.992 THI points per m/s of wind and adds 0.0068 THI points per W/m2 of solar radiation. Cowtrol uses the solar term (0.0068 per W/m2) for its solar correction.

Source: Mader et al. (2006), Environmental factors influencing heat stress in feedlot cattle (https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/animalscifacpub/608)
